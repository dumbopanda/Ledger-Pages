Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 September 2026

Ledger is a personal expense tracker built and run by an individual developer, not a company. This page explains, in plain language, what data Ledger collects, why, where it's kept, and how you can get it deleted.

What we collect

Only what's needed to run the app:

  • Your email address and Google account ID — from Google Sign-In, used to identify your account. Ledger never sees or stores a password.
  • The entries you create — amounts, accounts, categories, names, notes and dates you enter into the app.

That's the complete list. No phone number, no name beyond what Google supplies, no device fingerprinting, no location, no contacts or photos.

Why we collect it

Solely to operate the ledger you use: to show your entries back to you, keep your accounts and balances correct, and sync your data between your devices. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond running the app you asked for.

Ledger has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs of any kind.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored on Supabase, a database provider, hosted in a data center in India (AWS's Mumbai / ap-south-1 region). All traffic between the app and this server is encrypted in transit.

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not share it with any third party except the infrastructure providers strictly needed to run the app (Supabase for storage, Sentry for crash reports — see below, and Google for sign-in). None of these providers is permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, a technical error report is sent to Sentry, a crash-monitoring service, so bugs can be found and fixed. These reports never contain your amounts, entry names, notes, or any other financial data — that information is deliberately stripped out before anything is sent.

Your rights, and how to delete your data

You can:

  • See and correct your data at any time, directly in the app — it's your ledger, shown to you in full.
  • Export a copy of your data as a file, from Settings → Backup → Save a backup file.
  • Delete your account and all your data, immediately and permanently, either in the app (Settings → Delete account) or from this public page: dumbopanda.github.io/Ledger-Pages/delete-account. Deletion removes your entries, your accounts, your profile, and your Google sign-in link, and cannot be undone.
If you're in India, these rights are also protected under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). You can raise a complaint or request about your data at any time using the contact address below — that's your named grievance contact for this app.

Security

Your data is protected by row-level access rules on the database itself, so only your signed-in account can ever read or write your own entries — not other users, and not even a misconfigured feature of the app. There is no password to steal: sign-in is handled entirely by Google.

Children's privacy

Ledger is not directed at children and is not knowingly used to collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child's data has been collected, contact us below and it will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the "Last updated" date at the top will change, and material changes will be reflected in the app itself.
