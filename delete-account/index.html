This deletes your entries, your accounts, your profile, and your sign-in. It happens immediately and cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

  • All your entries
  • Your accounts
  • Your profile
  • Your sign-in (Google or email)
Want to keep a copy? Before deleting, open Ledger on your phone and go to Settings → Your own copy → Save file.

Sign in the way you use Ledger: with Google, or with a code sent to your email.
