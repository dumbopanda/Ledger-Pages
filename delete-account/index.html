Delete your account

This deletes your entries, your accounts, your profile, and your Google sign-in link. It happens immediately and cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

  • All your entries
  • Your accounts
  • Your profile
  • Google sign-in link
Want to keep a copy? Before deleting, open Ledger on your phone and go to Settings → Backup → Save a backup file.

Sign in with the Google account you use with Ledger to continue.

Type DELETE to confirm

This is the last step. It cannot be undone.
